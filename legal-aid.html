    Free legal help

    Free & low-cost legal aid finder

    Official and non-profit sources of free legal help, organised by country. If you qualify, these services can represent or advise you at little or no cost.

    United States

    • Legal Services Corporation — find legal aidFederally funded civil legal aid, searchable by location.
    • LawHelp.orgState-by-state directory of non-profit legal aid and self-help guides.
    • ABA Free Legal AnswersFree online civil legal questions answered by volunteer lawyers (eligibility applies).
    • LawHelp InteractiveFree guided interviews that complete court forms.
    • USA.gov — legal aidOfficial federal guide to free and low-cost legal help.
    • Cornell LIIFree access to US federal and state law.

    Canada

    • Legal Aid OntarioOntario's legal aid programme for eligible residents.
    • Commission des services juridiques (Québec)Legal aid for eligible Quebec residents.
    • ÉducaloiPlain-language legal information for Quebec.
    • Legal Aid BCLegal aid and family-law resources in British Columbia.
    • Department of Justice — legal aidFederal overview of legal aid across provinces and territories.
    • CanLIIFree Canadian case law and legislation.

    United Kingdom

    • Check if you can get legal aid (GOV.UK)Official eligibility checker for England and Wales.
    • Citizens AdviceFree, confidential advice on work, housing, debt and more.
    • Find a legal adviser (GOV.UK)Search legal aid providers.
    • LawWorksPro bono legal advice clinics.
    • Scottish Legal Aid BoardLegal aid in Scotland.

    India

    • National Legal Services Authority (NALSA)Free legal services under the Legal Services Authorities Act.
    • Tele-Law (Department of Justice)Free pre-litigation advice through video and phone.
    • eCourts ServicesCase status and court information.
    • India CodeOfficial repository of central and state Acts.

    Australia

    • National Legal AidLinks to legal aid commissions in each state and territory.
    • Community Legal Centres AustraliaFind a free community legal centre.
    • Legal Aid NSWLegal aid for New South Wales residents.
    • Victoria Legal AidLegal aid for Victorian residents.

    International

    • UNHCR — help for refugeesCountry-specific information for refugees and asylum seekers.
    • International Legal FoundationPublic defence for people who cannot afford a lawyer in several countries.
    • Pro bono clearing houses (TrustLaw)Connects NGOs and social enterprises with free legal assistance.

    Links go to independent organisations. Smart.Legal is not affiliated with them. Don't qualify for legal aid? Request a free case review.
