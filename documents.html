    How to use these templates well
    

    A good legal letter is specific, calm and dated. State the facts in order, cite the agreement or rule you are relying on, say exactly what you want and give a reasonable deadline. Keep a copy and send it in a way you can prove — email with a read receipt, tracked post or a courier.
    

    For agreements, both parties should read every clause, fill in all blanks and sign and date each copy. If a contract involves significant money, property, employees or intellectual property, a short review by a lawyer is inexpensive insurance.
