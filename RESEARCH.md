## Patterns that matter most for revenue

1. **Case-intake lead forms are the money engine** (LegalMatch, UpCounsel, Avvo, FindLaw chat). Smart.Legal makes the Free Case Review the primary CTA on every page.
2. **Free tools attract the traffic** (LegalZoom, Lawpath calculators, DoNotPay letters, FreeWill). Smart.Legal ships 6 calculators + 12 document generators + an issue navigator.
3. **Plain-language guides satisfy AdSense "valuable content"** (Nolo, Educaloi, Citizens Advice). Smart.Legal ships 12 long-form original guides + a 120+ term dictionary.
4. **Directories monetize the lawyer side** (Martindale, Super Lawyers, LII). Smart.Legal adds a "For Lawyers" partner/lead-buyer page.
5. **Nonprofit-style donations work for free tools** (LII, Educaloi, CRS, LawHelp Interactive). Smart.Legal adds donation tiers, sponsorship, contests and hiring.
6. **Auto-updating news/trackers keep the site fresh** (Justia, Above the Law, Law.com). Smart.Legal refreshes news + videos daily via GitHub Actions.
7. **Every major player states clearly it is not a law firm** (LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, Priori, UpCounsel). Smart.Legal does the same on every page.
