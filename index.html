    Frequently asked questions

    Is Smart.Legal free?

    Yes. Guides, tools, calculators, the dictionary and case-review requests are free for visitors.

    No. Smart.Legal provides legal information and connects visitors with independent attorneys. It does not give legal advice.

    Who will contact me after a case review?

    Independent attorneys or legal-service partners who handle your type of matter in your area may contact you. You are never obliged to hire anyone.
