    Privacy Policy

    Last updated November 22, 2026

    This Privacy Policy explains how Smart.Legal ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit smart.legal (the "Site").

    1. Information you give us

    When you submit a form — free case review, legal question, newsletter, donation pledge, contest entry, job application, partner or contact form — we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, location and description of your matter. Do not submit highly sensitive information you do not want shared with the people who will respond to your request.

    2. How we use it

    • To respond to your request and, for case reviews, to share it with independent attorneys, legal-service partners or legal-aid organisations that may be able to help you in your area.
    • To send newsletters you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
    • To run contests, process applications, sponsorships and donation pledges.
    • To improve and secure the Site and create anonymous content from questions.

    We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing case-review details with partners at your request is part of the service you asked for.

    3. Tools run in your browser

    The Navigator, document generator, calculators, dictionary search and quiz process what you type locally in your browser. That information is not sent to us unless you submit a form.

    4. Form processing

    Forms are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-relay service, which processes submissions on our behalf to deliver them by email.

    5. Cookies and advertising

    We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences (such as dark mode), measure use of the Site and show advertising.

    • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
    • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
    • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
    • Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

    Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message where required before personalised ads are served.

    6. Embedded content

    Videos are embedded from YouTube using privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. News headlines link to external publishers whose own policies apply.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

    8. Retention and security

    We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

    9. Children

    The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in some regions), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

    10. Changes

    We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

    11. Contact

    Questions? Use our contact form.
