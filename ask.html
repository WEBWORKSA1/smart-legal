    Before you ask

    Many questions are already answered in our legal guides and dictionary. For time-sensitive matters — a court date, an eviction notice, an arrest — do not wait for a published answer: use the free case review or contact legal aid now.

    Good questions include

    • Your country and state or province
    • A short timeline of what happened
    • What outcome you want
    • Any deadline you have been given
