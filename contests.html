    Contests & prizes

    Learn the law. Win prizes.

    Smart.Legal runs regular contests that reward people for learning and explaining the law clearly.

    Know-Your-Rights Challenge

    Monthly

    Score 10/10 on our quiz, then answer a tie-breaker question in 50 words or less. The most accurate, clearest answers win.

    Prize pool funded by sponsors — winners receive gift cards and featured recognition; amounts are announced at the start of each round.

    Plain-Language Law Essay Award

    Quarterly

    Explain one legal concept to a 15-year-old in 600–900 words. Open to students and writers aged 18+.

    Top essay is published on Smart.Legal with a cash award, plus runner-up prizes when sponsored.

    Legal-Tech Idea Sprint

    Twice a year

    Pitch a free tool that would help people solve a legal problem. Winning ideas may be built into Smart.Legal.

    Winner receives a cash prize and a paid build collaboration when the round is sponsored.

    • No purchase or donation necessary. A donation does not improve your chances of winning.
    • Open to individuals aged 18+ where contests are lawful. Void where prohibited or restricted.
